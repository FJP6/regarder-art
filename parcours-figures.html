Bringing Figures to Life

From Giotto to Titian, discover how Renaissance artists gave figures emotions, gestures, movement and an inner life.

Giving Birth to Emotion

Faces and bodies no longer merely signify; they make us feel the drama.

Giotto

The Lamentation of Christ

Bodies gather around Christ. Faces and hands convey a shared grief.
