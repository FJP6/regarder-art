Why does Jesus give Peter the keys?

In Matthew’s Gospel, Jesus asks his disciples who they believe he is. Simon answers that he is the Messiah, the Son of the living God.

Jesus then calls him Peter, declares that he will build his Church upon this rock, and promises him the keys of the kingdom of heaven.

These keys do not open an ordinary door. They represent spiritual responsibility: Peter is entrusted with guiding the community of believers.

Perugino makes this promise visible. Peter kneels to receive the keys while the surrounding figures witness the transfer. In the Sistine Chapel, the message carries particular significance: the popes regard themselves as Peter’s successors.

The detail to find

Two keys appear between Christ’s and Peter’s hands: this small object carries the meaning of the entire fresco.

**Source: Gospel of Matthew, 16:13–19.**
