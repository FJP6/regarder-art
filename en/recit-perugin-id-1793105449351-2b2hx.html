Why does Jesus give Peter the keys?

In Matthew’s Gospel, Simon recognises Jesus as the Messiah. Jesus replies by linking Peter’s name with the rock on which he will build his Church. He also promises him the keys of the kingdom of heaven.

Perugino gives that promise a visible form. Look at the small gap between Christ standing and Peter kneeling. The painted keys identify the moment in the story.

Detail to find

The keys. In the foreground, place the label between Christ’s hand and Peter’s, beside the two keys.
