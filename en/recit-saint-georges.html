Saint George and the Dragon — A monster on a leash

Why is the princess holding the dragon on a leash while the knight is still fighting it?

This is the famous Saint George slaying the dragon. His battle with the monster belongs to a Christian legend, rather than a biblical story.

According to the Golden Legend, a dragon terrorises a town. To appease it, the inhabitants offer animals, then human victims chosen by lot. One day, the lot falls on the king’s daughter.

George arrives as the princess awaits her fate. He confronts the dragon and wounds it. Then he asks the young woman to tie her belt around the monster’s neck: now tame, it follows her back to the town.

George promises the inhabitants that he will kill the dragon if they convert to Christianity. They agree, and he slays the beast.

Look at the painting

Uccello brings two successive moments together: the battle and the dragon already subdued. That is why the princess can calmly hold its leash while George attacks!

The strange cave, the monster’s decorative wings and the spiralling cloud give the legend the atmosphere of a magical tale.

Source: Jacobus de Voragine, The Golden Legend, the story of Saint George.
National Gallery collection record.
