Why Does Francis Receive Christ’s Wounds ?

According to Franciscan accounts, Francis withdrew to Mount La Verna to pray in 1224. A seraph associated with the crucified Christ appeared to him, and he received the marks of the Passion on his own body.

These marks are called the stigmata. For believers, they express the intensity of his identification with Christ. The image makes a spiritual experience visible, rather than showing an ordinary event.

Find the elements connecting the saint to the apparition. Then consider the landscape: the miraculous narrative and the visible world coexist within one fresco.

This episode belongs to Franciscan religious tradition.
