What is happening before our eyes?

The Baptism of Christ — When heaven opens

Why does Jesus receive baptism from John the Baptist?

On the banks of the Jordan, John the Baptist calls people to change their lives. Those who come to him receive a baptism in water as a sign of repentance.

Jesus comes forward in turn. John is surprised: he feels that he should be the one receiving baptism from Jesus. But Jesus insists: this act must fulfil God’s will.

After the baptism, the heavens open. The Spirit of God descends like a dove, and a voice from heaven identifies Jesus as his beloved Son. The story thus reveals who it is that John has just baptised.

Look at the painting

Piero gives this event an almost perfect stillness. Jesus stands at the centre with his hands joined in prayer. Above him, the dove continues the axis of his body: it represents the Holy Spirit.

Behind John, a man removes his clothes, perhaps to receive baptism himself. This everyday gesture brings the scene closer to us.

In Piero’s painting, divine revelation takes place in a calm, luminous and deeply human world.

Biblical reference : Matthew 3, 13–17.
