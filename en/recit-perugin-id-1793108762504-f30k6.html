Three images to distinguish

The Italian word “pietà” evokes pity and compassion. In Christian art, a Pietà shows Mary holding the body of her dead son, sometimes surrounded by other figures.

This is not an episode narrated as such in the Gospels. It is a devotional image inviting reflection on Christ’s death and his mother’s grief.

Three images to distinguish

In a Descent from the Cross, Christ’s body is taken down from the cross.

In a Pietà, the action pauses: Mary and those close to Christ mourn around his body.

In an Entombment, the body is carried to or laid in its tomb.

In Perugino’s painting, hands support the body and gazes turn downwards. Everything invites us to remain beside those who mourn.
