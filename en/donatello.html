Donatello

c. 1386–1466

Bringing sculpture to life. Donatello turns bodies, faces and sculpted space into a profoundly human experience.

In a few words

A Florentine who transformed sculpture
Born in Florence around 1386, Donatello trained in the artistic circle of Lorenzo Ghiberti. Close to Brunelleschi, he worked in marble, bronze and wood. Active in Florence and Padua, he made sculpture an art of movement, emotion and depth. He died in Florence in 1466.

How does Donatello bring sculpture to life?
Convincing bodies, expressive gestures, faces that are not always idealized, and astonishingly delicate low relief: stiacciato.

05 · GO FURTHER · COMPARE

Two crucifixes, two visions of Christ

Look closely

Notice the position of the arms, the tilt of the head and the treatment of the torso. How does the portrayal of suffering differ?

Understanding

Only a few years apart, two Florentine artists offered different visions of Christ’s body.

Key takeaways

Donatello gives human figures a new sense of presence. He explores the truth of the body, the intensity of emotion and spatial depth, even in almost flat relief.
