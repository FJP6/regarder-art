Why does this couple embrace with such tenderness?

According to Christian tradition, Joachim and Anne are Mary’s parents, and therefore Jesus’s grandparents. But their story begins with a long wait: they have no children.

When Joachim’s offering is rejected because he has no offspring, he withdraws in sorrow far from home. Left alone, Anne mourns both her husband’s absence and their disappointed hopes.

Yet an angel tells each of them that their prayers have been heard: they will have a child. Joachim returns, and Anne runs to meet him. Their embrace celebrates their reunion and the promise of a birth: Mary’s.

**Look at the fresco**

Giotto tells this joyful story through bodies. Anne draws Joachim towards her, their faces meet and their hands complete the embrace. The sacred story takes the form of a deeply familiar gesture.

The city gate frames their meeting. But what touches us first is this couple reunited after sorrow and separation.
