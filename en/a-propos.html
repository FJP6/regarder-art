The spirit of Regarder l’Art

This website grew out of the joy I find in visiting museums, exploring cities and stopping in front of a work of art. And out of a wish: to better understand what I am looking at, then share what I discover.

It also grew out of a frustration: the fear of missing what a work of art might reveal or make me feel. Why that gesture, that light, that unusual perspective? What changes from one artist to another?

That is why I want to offer simple ways into works of art, without prescribing how to see them. A few points of reference that leave everyone free to look, to feel and to be moved.

Art can also be discovered through play. Here you will find humour, knowing winks, hidden works and small details that are easy to overlook. Sometimes a more mischievous or sensual gaze invites us to linger: artists have much to tell us, including in places where we do not always dare to look.

That freedom goes hand in hand with care for the artworks, the explanations and the sources.

The learning journeys and artist profiles sit alongside my travel journals: personal impressions, encounters and surprises I want to share.

Regarder l’Art will grow as I make new discoveries. I hope it inspires you to open a door, step closer to a painting or see a familiar work in a new light.

Learn to see, dare to look.

Jérôme
