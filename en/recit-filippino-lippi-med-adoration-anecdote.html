The Day Filippino Lippi Took Leonardo da Vinci’s Place

Florence, 1481. The monks of San Donato a Scopeto commission Leonardo da Vinci to paint an Adoration of the Magi for the high altar of their church.

But Leonardo has other plans. He leaves for Milan, abandoning the painting unfinished.

The monks wait for his return. In vain.

The years go by… and still no painting!

Eventually, they lose patience and entrust the commission to Filippino Lippi.

In 1496, he delivers his Adoration of the Magi, completed, signed and dated.

The irony? Today, both paintings hang in the Uffizi Gallery in Florence: the one Leonardo never finished and the one Filippino painted in his place.

It just goes to show that even a genius can leave his clients wanting more!

This story is documented by the commission records and the Uffizi Galleries’ official descriptions. However, there is no evidence to suggest that a personal rivalry existed between the two painters.
