A commission taken up by Filippino

In 1481, the Augustinian canons of Sant’Agostino commissioned an Adoration of the Magi from Leonardo for the high altar of San Donato a Scopeto. The panel, now in the Uffizi, remained unfinished.

In the 1490s, the canons commissioned a new altarpiece from Filippino Lippi. He signed and dated his work 29 March 1496, as the inscription on the reverse records.

Both paintings are now in the Uffizi. Seen together, they place an unfinished composition, which reveals an ongoing process of invention, beside a completed altarpiece made for worship. The documents establish the sequence of commissions; they do not prove a rivalry between the two artists.
