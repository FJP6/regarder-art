Did the painter close his eyes?

Look at the second soldier from the left: he wears brown armour and rests his head against the edge of the tomb.

According to an old tradition, Piero della Francesca gave this soldier his own features. The identification remains disputed: this is a possible self-portrait, rather than an established fact.

Yet the idea has a delightful twist: the painter may have slipped his face into the scene… as a man sleeping through the painting’s most extraordinary event!
