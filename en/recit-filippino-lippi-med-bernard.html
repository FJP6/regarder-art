When the Virgin Appears to a Monk

Saint Bernard of Clairvaux, a 12th-century monk, is known for his profound devotion to the Virgin Mary.

According to tradition, while meditating on the Scriptures, Mary appears to him, surrounded by angels. She comes to reward his faith and inspire his writings.

But look closely: the Virgin does not appear as a distant, unapproachable queen. She approaches the monk with tenderness, while the angels watch this extraordinary encounter.

Filippino Lippi thus transforms a mystical vision into an almost intimate scene: for a brief moment, the heavenly world seems to enter the world of humankind.
