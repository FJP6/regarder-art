What are the men on the right showing?

Look at the small objects being shown to the right of Christ’s body. They are the crown of thorns and the nails. The figure holding them has been tentatively identified as Palla Strozzi, who commissioned the altarpiece for the sacristy of Santa Trinita.

The sacristy also housed his family’s burial chapel. Holy Week services took place there. The 2025 exhibition catalogue links Mary Magdalene’s gesture as she bends to kiss Christ’s feet to the veneration of the cross on Good Friday. Look again, first to the right and then to the left. The objects on display and Magdalene’s gesture suggest what worshippers could see and do in front of this altarpiece.
