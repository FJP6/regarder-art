These icons have a story to tell

Click them whenever they appear beside an artwork.

A curious glance…

A sensual or playful detail, a different viewpoint: the keyhole eye invites you to take a closer look.

What is happening before our eyes?

The book reveals the story behind the scene: who are the figures, what is happening, and which details help us understand it?

Zoom in: take time to look

A face, a gesture, a texture or a subtle detail can change the way we read an artwork.

  1. Click the imageThe artwork opens in a larger view.
  2. Zoom, then dragExplore the details at your own pace.
  3. Return to the whole workUse “Full view” to see the entire composition again.

On a phone: pinch with two fingers to zoom, then drag the image to move around.

On a computer: use the mouse wheel or the + / − buttons, then click and drag to explore.

The red cross closes the image; on a computer, the Escape key works too.
