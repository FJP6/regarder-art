Why do they meet at the city gate?

In the full fresco, Joachim comes from the left, accompanied by a shepherd partly cut off by the frame. This detail recalls the time he spent among shepherds. In a tradition drawn from the Protoevangelium of James, Joachim withdraws from home after his offering is rejected. He and Anne, who have long hoped for a child, learn that they will have a daughter, Mary.

Look again at the edges of the fresco. The shepherd remains at the edge, on the side Joachim has come from. Anne has already stepped away from the women on the city side. Between them, the kiss brings together the two paths that the story had set apart.
