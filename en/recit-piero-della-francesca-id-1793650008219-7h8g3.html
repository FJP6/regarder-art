The geometry of a suspended moment

The dove, the bowl and Christ’s praying hands align along one vertical. This line brings together the essential elements of the baptism and places Jesus at the heart of the composition.

2 · The echo of the tree — green
The pale trunk repeats Christ’s upright stance and echoes his skin tone. Tree and body mirror one another, giving the scene a stable structure.

3 · Foreshortened forms — blue
The dove’s outstretched wings form a horizontal that balances the verticals. Its body is foreshortened, as though it were flying towards us. Seen at an angle, the bowl has an oval outline: Piero makes its volume perceptible.

4 · Figures becoming smaller — orange
Compare John the Baptist in the foreground, the man removing his tunic behind him and the more distant figures. Their decreasing size and overlapping positions lead us into the landscape. The orange brackets help compare the two background groups.

Takeaway
Piero combines ordered lines with a deep landscape. Geometry contributes to the painting’s sense of calm and balance.

The annotations are approximate visual guides; they do not reconstruct a system of vanishing points.
