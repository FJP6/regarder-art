Inside a Florentine Workshop

The Birth of the Virgin covers a broad stretch of wall. At Santa Maria Novella, look at where it ends, then look up at the episodes stacked above it. A chapel of this size called for a team to share the work of designing and painting the scenes.

In Florence, a bottega was both a team of painters and a place to learn. Ghirlandaio worked in the Tornabuoni Chapel with his brothers and other collaborators. The basilica notes that collaboration is especially apparent in the higher, more dimly lit lunettes. Michelangelo’s time in the workshop is documented. Identifying a particular figure as his work is a separate question.

Return to the fresco and pick out the repeated horizontal lines, from the furnishings to the frieze. Then consider the decisions a team had to make together to give scenes painted at different heights a sense of unity.
