When the Virgin Appears to a Monk

Bernard of Clairvaux, a twelfth-century monk, is known for his deep devotion to the Virgin Mary.

According to tradition, as he meditates on the Scriptures, Mary appears to him surrounded by angels. She comes to reward his faith and inspire his writings.

But look closely: the Virgin does not appear as an unapproachable queen. She approaches the monk gently, while the angels watch this extraordinary encounter.

Filippino Lippi thus transforms a mystical vision into an almost intimate scene: for a moment, the heavenly world seems to enter the world of human beings.
