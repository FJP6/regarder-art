The Medici: how bankers became the masters of Florence

Before becoming a dynasty of princes, the Medici were a family of bankers .

Their wealth and networks gave them considerable influence in Florence, a city that still retained its republican institutions. Cosimo the Elder consolidated this power in the fifteenth century. Supporting religious buildings, artists and scholars expressed devotion and taste, while leaving the family’s mark on the city. His grandson Lorenzo, known as the Magnificent , became another central figure in Florentine life. Poetry, ancient learning and artistic creation met around the Medici.

Botticelli worked within this world, where images could celebrate faith, beauty and prestige. In the Adoration of the Magi , the patron Gaspare del Lama honours the family by gathering its members around Jesus.

The Medici also belonged to the Company of the Magi and took part in Florence’s Epiphany celebrations. The painting shows how a family could enter history… by entering a sacred story.
