The Day Filippino Lippi Replaced Leonardo da Vinci

Florence, 1481. The monks of San Donato a Scopeto commission Leonardo da Vinci to paint an Adoration of the Magi for the high altar of their church.

But Leonardo has other plans. He leaves for Milan, abandoning the painting unfinished.

The monks wait for him to return. In vain.

Years go by… and still no painting!

Eventually, they lose patience and entrust the commission to Filippino Lippi.

In 1496, Filippino delivers his Adoration of the Magi, completed, signed, and dated.

The twist? Today, both paintings are in the Uffizi Galleries in Florence: the one Leonardo never finished and the one Filippino painted in his place.

Even a genius, it seems, can leave his clients waiting!

The story is documented by records of the commission and the Uffizi Galleries’ catalogue entries. There is, however, no evidence of a personal rivalry between the two painters.
